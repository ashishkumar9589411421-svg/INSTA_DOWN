Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Data Collection

We believe in minimalism. In.dl collects only the bare minimum required:

  • IP Addresses: Used strictly for rate limiting (5 downloads/day for guests) and abuse prevention.
  • User Accounts: If you register, we use Supabase to securely store your authentication data.
  • Payment Proofs: Screenshots uploaded for plan upgrades are stored securely in Supabase Storage.

2. File Retention

We do not store your downloaded files permanently.

  • When you request a download, our server temporarily processes the Instagram media.
  • Automatic Deletion: All downloaded files are automatically wiped from our servers within a short background interval.
  • We do not keep logs of what specific content you download.

3. Authentication & Cookies

We use Supabase Authentication. Authentication tokens are stored securely in your browser's local storage to keep you logged in. We do not use third-party tracking pixels for advertising purposes.

4. Contact

If you have privacy concerns or wish to request data deletion, please contact the admin via the support form.
